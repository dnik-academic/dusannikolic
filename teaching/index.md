## Teaching

My teaching interests include phonetics, phonology,
speech perception, and spoken language processing.

I have taught courses at the undergraduate and
graduate levels, covering theoretical and experimental
approaches to the study of speech and language.

## Courses

### Phonetics

Introduction to the acoustic and articulatory properties
of speech sounds.

### Introduction to Linguistics

Introduction to the sound systems of human languages.
